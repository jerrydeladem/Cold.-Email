How it works
  1. On the Write tab, say what you offer and who you are writing to.
  2. Pick a format and tone, then tap Write my email.
  3. Fine-tune it with the buttons below the result, check it for spam words, then copy it or open it in your email app.

Tip: use the Summarize tab on a bio first for a more personal result.
